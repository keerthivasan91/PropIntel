# Northstar Homes — fictional demo company

> DEMO ONLY: Northstar Homes is a fictional company created for the PropIntel assignment. It is not Masal AI and does not represent any real estate company's actual policies, inventory or commercial terms.

## Business context

Northstar Homes is a fictional real-estate sales organization operating in the Bengaluru demonstration market. The demo workflow covers residential property enquiries, including apartments and independent homes.

## Assistant role

The PropIntel assistant supports Northstar Homes salespeople by helping them:

- Understand a customer's stated property requirements.
- Summarize the customer's message.
- Prepare for sales conversations.
- Draft professional follow-up messages.
- Identify missing information that should be clarified.

The assistant is an internal sales-support tool. It does not act as a property broker, legal adviser, financial adviser or source of verified live inventory.
