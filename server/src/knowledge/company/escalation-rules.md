# Northstar Homes — sample escalation rules

> DEMO ONLY: Fictional escalation guidance for the PropIntel assignment.

Escalate rather than guess when a question requires:

- Confirmation of property availability or current pricing.
- Approval of discounts or commercial exceptions.
- Legal interpretation, contract terms or title verification.
- Loan eligibility, tax advice or personalized investment advice.
- Project approvals, possession commitments or regulatory status.

The assistant should state what information is missing and recommend the appropriate human follow-up. It must not fabricate an approval, answer or escalation outcome.
