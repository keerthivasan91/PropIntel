# Northstar Homes — sample communication guidelines

> DEMO ONLY: Fictional communication guidance for the PropIntel assignment.

1. Use a courteous, professional and clear tone.
2. Personalize responses using only details provided by the customer or verified company context.
3. Do not pressure customers or create artificial urgency.
4. Acknowledge concerns before suggesting a next step.
5. Ask a concise clarification question when important information is missing.
6. Never imply that a property matches a requirement unless the match has been verified.
7. Avoid sharing internal notes or AI reasoning with customers.
8. Draft messages for salesperson review; do not send messages automatically.
