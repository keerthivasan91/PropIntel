# Northstar Homes — sample sales policies

> DEMO ONLY: These are illustrative rules written for the PropIntel assignment. They are not Masal AI policies or verified industry rules.

## Policy version

- Document ID: northstar-sales-policies
- Version: 1.0
- Status: Fictional demonstration policy

## Rules

1. Do not promise a discount, price concession or special offer unless an authorized person has confirmed it.
2. Do not claim that a property is available unless current availability has been verified by an authorized source.
3. Do not invent property prices, amenities, possession dates, approvals or project details.
4. If pricing or inventory information is missing, tell the salesperson that it needs confirmation.
5. Do not make guarantees about appreciation, rental returns or investment performance.
6. Do not present an AI-generated lead score as a probability that a customer will purchase.
7. Keep the salesperson in control. AI recommendations are suggestions and must be reviewed before use.
8. Escalate legal, contractual, loan and tax questions to an appropriately qualified or authorized representative.
